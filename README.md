Customer Churn Analysis

<h1>Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard</h1>

<p>This project analyzes customer churn patterns using an interactive Power BI dashboard. It explores customer demographics, geographic regions, contract types, payment methods, internet services, and churn categories to identify potential reasons for customer attrition and recommend strategies for improving customer retention.</p>

<h2>Project Objectives</h2>

<ul>
<li>Analyze the overall customer churn rate and key performance indicators.</li>
<li>Identify customer segments with higher churn rates.</li>
<li>Evaluate the relationship between contract types and customer retention.</li>
<li>Understand churn patterns across payment methods and internet services.</li>
<li>Compare churn rates across geographic regions.</li>
<li>Provide actionable recommendations to improve customer retention.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Age Group Analysis</h3>

<ul>

<li>Customers below 21 account for approximately 0.5K churned customers.</li>
</ul>

<p>The above-65 age group appears to be an important segment for further investigation. Customer satisfaction surveys, service support improvements, and age-appropriate retention initiatives could help determine whether specific needs contribute to churn.</p>

<h2>9. Business Recommendations</h2>

<h3>Short-Term Strategies</h3>

<ul>
<li>Identify high-risk month-to-month customers and offer relevant retention incentives.</li>
<li>Investigate service complaints among Fiber Optic customers.</li>
<li>Analyze competitor-related cancellation feedback.</li>
<li>Review billing friction and payment convenience.</li>
<li>Monitor customer satisfaction across high-churn geographic regions.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Long-Term Strategies</h3>

<ul>
<li>Develop customer segmentation based on demographics, contract type, services, and payment methods.</li>
<li>Build a predictive churn model to identify customers at risk of leaving.</li>
<li>Create a regularly updated customer retention dashboard.</li>
<li>Use customer feedback to prioritize product and service improvements.</li>
<li>Measure retention campaign performance against a baseline or control group.</li>
</ul>

<h2>11. Conclusion</h2>

<p>The Customer Churn Analysis dashboard reports an overall churn rate of 26.99% across 77,016 customers.
